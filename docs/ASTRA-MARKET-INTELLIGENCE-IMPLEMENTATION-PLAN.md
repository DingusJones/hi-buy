## 1. Executive summary and product principles

Build **hi buy.com**, a mobile-first, private research workspace with four separate primary sections: **Stocks**, **ETFs**, **Commodities**, and **Crypto**. Stocks and ETFs provide discovery and research candidates; commodities provide macro/benchmark context; crypto provides at least BTC, ETH, and SOL prices. Bring discovery, explainable rankings, charts, official documents, notes, watchlists, alerts, and a local paper ledger into one place. Dividend and Growth are independent ETF tags. All values expose source, observation time, delay, applicability, and calculation version. Scores measure alignment with a documented research method, never probability of profit.

The recommended first release uses end-of-day (EOD) stock/ETF research over two versioned universes: 60 liquid common stocks and 40 ordinary ETFs. Each has four independent top-10 boards (gainers, losers, share volume, and dollar volume where permitted), labeled “Top within tracked universe.” Publish **Top 5 Stock Research Candidates** and **Top 5 ETF Research Candidates** independently, with five qualifying entries when possible and honest vacant slots otherwise. Each area has its own success, degraded, or data-unavailable status. Never substitute another asset type or yesterday's candidate to fill a slot. Jay's revision explicitly authorizes fewer than five when gates fail; no further cardinality decision is required.

The overview links to all four sections, displays “5 Stock Candidates” and “5 ETF Candidates” as separate groups, and includes **Top 5 Market News**. Stock/ETF detail pages and relevant candidate cards expose instrument-filtered news links plus a separate official filing/release group. News is attributed context, not a hidden input to technical scores. Crypto uses a 24/7 rolling-24-hour profile, commodities use source-specific observation periods, and neither enters stock/ETF rankings. Separate routes, filters, typed APIs, methodology profiles and health states enforce these boundaries.

Free data is not automatically licensed for public display. A credible private version is feasible using a free-key market-data adapter plus SEC facts and permitted issuer information; a continuously updated, consolidated, publicly redistributable, no-key market terminal is not established by this research. Live activation depends on verified access and applicable display/storage rights. Until then, fixtures and explicitly dated, legally retained replay data run the same pipeline. No paid dependency is silently introduced.

Use React/TypeScript with Next.js, a TypeScript domain calculation package, PostgreSQL, a separate durable worker, and Apache ECharts using the app's permitted data. Deploy privately on the ThinkCentre initially. Favor one calculation implementation shared by production, replay, and backtests. TradingView is an outbound research destination, not a market-data provider. There is no order execution, brokerage integration, personalized portfolio advice, or promise of daily income.

Estimated full private MVP: 59–84 engineering days for one experienced full-time engineer, plus 10–20% contingency and external licensing response time. The smallest useful slice takes roughly 5–8 days and is part of that estimate: one stock and one ETF, dated replay, daily chart and indicators, source drawer, TradingView links, notes/watchlist, and a paper entry. Data subscription budget remains $0; hardware, electricity, optional hosting, domains, and messaging are separate costs.

## 3. Scope: MVP vs later phases

**Private MVP:** separate 60-stock/40-ETF tracked universes; eight separately titled EOD top-10 boards (dollar-volume boards capability-gated); two independent five-slot research shortlists; Stocks/ETFs/Commodities/Crypto navigation; permitted commodity benchmarks; BTC/ETH/SOL prices; Top 5 Market News and per-asset News; all daily indicators defined below; weekly confluence where history permits; Dividend/Growth/Core/Bond/Sector/International/Thematic ETF tags; stock screener; saved screens; watchlists; up to four-asset comparison; complete asset research workspace; editable notes/thesis/scenarios; paper ledger; EOD in-app alerts; source health; methodology and evaluation report; centralized external links. Fundamental fields may honestly be missing; no premium estimates or news subscription; missing permitted news coverage must be explicit.

**Conditional MVP extension:** delayed intraday board and 60-minute charts/alerts when permitted consolidated bars, request budget, and session completeness pass validation. UI must remain useful when this capability is absent. Intraday is not needed for EOD acceptance.

**Later:** broader universe, international instruments and FX, more issuer adapters and historical ETF holdings, finer intraday evaluation, opt-in push/email, public service only with rights, portfolio-level paper analytics, additional strategy families, optional on-chain read-only adapter. Bond-specific ranking requires duration/credit data and separate methodology. No on-chain discovery promises until free legally usable yield coverage is proven.

**Not an MVP shortcut:** an unlabeled synthetic dashboard, proprietary data scraped from websites, a universal “yield” field merging SEC yield and APY, or a historical backtest that uses today's constituents without disclosure.

### Stocks

`/stocks` contains individual listed stocks only, with `/stocks/boards` and `/stocks/candidates`. Stock filters persist in a `stocks` namespace and saved screens reject ETF-only metrics. Show stock universe, four board headings, Top 5 Stock Research Candidates, `stock-v1` methodology and stock health. Detail pages have News and Official filings/releases groups; missing coverage displays “News unavailable,” never an empty implication of no events.

### ETFs

`/etfs`, `/etfs/boards`, and `/etfs/candidates` have independent filters, snapshot state and `etf-v1` methodology. Show four ETF board headings and Top 5 ETF Research Candidates. Dividend, Growth, Core/Broad Market, Bond/Income, Sector, Thematic, International and other tags are multi-label. Show AUM, expense/inception/type, holdings coverage and duplicate-exposure warnings. Commodity ETFs remain ETF listings in this section; they are not commodity spot or futures quotes.

### Commodities

`/commodities` and `/commodities/:id` use `commodity-context-v1`, separate health and filters for energy/metals/agriculture, spot/futures/benchmark, currency, unit, venue and observation frequency. Initial catalog: gold, silver, WTI crude, Brent crude, natural gas, copper, corn and wheat. Render permitted values or explicit unavailable cards with official source links; do not invent a free complete feed. MVP favors energy spot observations and dated benchmark series. Every card labels instrument type, quoted unit (e.g. USD/barrel or USD/troy ounce), period/time, source, delay, and session or “periodic benchmark—no trading session.” Futures additionally require exchange, contract month, expiry, multiplier and settlement-versus-last basis. Spot/benchmark contract fields are N/A. Continuous futures must name roll trigger, contracts, adjustment method and roll dates; disabled until permitted contract histories exist. Contango/backwardation only from synchronized same-unit comparable contracts: next minus near settlement, positive/negative respectively; otherwise unavailable. Never infer a futures curve from ETFs or monthly spot averages. Negative futures prices are possible; stock positive-price validators and log returns must not be reused blindly.

### Crypto

`/crypto` and `/crypto/:id` use `crypto-prices-v1` and separate health/filter state. Required cards: **BTC**, **ETH**, **SOL** prices in USD, rolling 24-hour percentage change, 24-hour quote-currency volume, market cap and liquidity/spread/depth if permitted, source observation time, retrieval time, delay, aggregate/venue coverage and source/explorer links. Missing optional fields are unknown, not zero. Identity uses provider IDs (`bitcoin`, `ethereum`, `solana`) plus chain identity, not ticker-only matching. Distinguish native assets from wrapped tokens and exchange pairs. Label aggregated prices as aggregates; venue fallbacks cannot inherit aggregate volume or market cap. No stock/ETF score, share-volume ranking or securities market-close convention applies. DeFi TVL/APR/APY later gets a separate metric family with chain/protocol/contract/risk labels.

Shared navigation never implies a common clock. Section filters, cache keys, URL query state and methodology links are independent. `/assets/:id` may redirect by canonical instrument type; it cannot render a mixed ranking. Watchlists may link across types but disable a combined score sort.

### Top 5 Market News and per-asset News

Overview displays up to five current, deduplicated linked stories with explicit five-slot shortage states. Each item has headline, publisher, publication time, canonical link, affected instruments/entities, category and one neutral relevance sentence (e.g. “Issuer announced a distribution for this fund”). Do not assert a headline caused a move without evidence. Asset News filters by verified entity linkage; candidate cards show up to two relevant links and link to complete asset News. Official filings/releases form a separately labeled primary-source group even when the same event has a news story. Paywalled items are labeled link-only. Display feed as-of, coverage, attribution and delayed/unavailable states. No article-body ingestion, copying, paywall bypass or remote publisher tracking pixels.


Primary journey: choose Dividend or Growth → inspect why a fund has that tag → compare like-for-like metrics → open asset → read source drawer and risk/context → save thesis and watchlist → record a paper plan → review later alerts and outcomes. Second journey: overview candidate → verify dated setup and uncertain event coverage → wait for confirmation or create paper intent → review invalidation/outcome. “Daily” describes publication cadence, not a duty to trade.

Asset workspace includes price/volume candlesticks, Fibonacci overlays, 20/50/100/200 SMA/EMA toggles, RSI/MACD/ADX/ATR/Bollinger/relative-strength panes, daily/weekly and capability-gated 60-minute timeframes. Default mobile view shows price/volume plus RSI; other panes are selectable to avoid an unreadable wall of plots. Chart settings and manual annotations are user-specific; manual pivots never enter official rankings.

Research tabs: fundamentals; ETF holdings/exposure and distributions; corporate actions and earnings/ex-dividend/record/payment dates; SEC/issuer documents; peer/benchmark/sector context and board rank; score explanation; notes/thesis and bull/base/bear scenarios; risk checklist; paper journal; alert history. Show event coverage as “unknown” when no calendar is available, not “no events.” News is official release links or explicitly licensed headlines, with publisher/time and no copied paywalled article body.

Research completeness is a checklist plus counts: required/optional fields known, delayed, stale, missing, conflicting, or inapplicable. Never translate it into investment confidence. A source drawer on every metric includes raw source URL or endpoint family (without secrets), observation/period/publication/retrieval times, feed, units, delay, adjustment basis, derivation/version, and competing values.

Mobile acceptance: 360 px viewport without page-level horizontal overflow; tables may scroll inside labeled regions, with card alternatives. ### External research link resolver

Create one registry keyed by canonical instrument ID and destination. Store provider exchange code, provider symbol alias, verified overview slug/URL, status, validity dates, last review, and provenance. MIC/exchange metadata and TradingView exchange codes are different namespaces. A confirmed AAPL mapping is `NASDAQ:AAPL`; TradingView currently uses `AMEX:SPY`, so do not blindly construct `NYSE:SPY` from a generic NYSE label. Verified public pages: [AAPL](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/NASDAQ-AAPL/) and [SPY](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/AMEX-SPY/).

Chart URL contract: `https://www.tradingview.com/chart/?symbol=` plus URL-encoded verified qualified symbol (for AAPL, `NASDAQ%3AAAPL`). Overview uses the separately verified `/symbols/NASDAQ-AAPL/` style slug; optional technical-analysis subpage only when explicitly registered. Do not infer punctuation conversion or a stable analysis URL for every asset. A chart deep link is best-effort, not a guaranteed external-service contract; manually check representative links at release without scraping their data.

Unknown mapping: show “TradingView mapping unavailable,” offer the external TradingView homepage plus copy-symbol/name, never route to an arbitrary same-ticker instrument. Delisted mappings are historical and visibly labeled; never substitute a successor without an explicit relationship. Share classes, preferred shares, renamed stocks, international listings and tokenized assets require independent aliases; chain+contract identity is never enough to invent a TradingView listing.

All external links use labeled icons, accessible “opens external service in new tab” text, `target=_blank`, `rel=noopener noreferrer`, HTTPS and destination allowlists. Official issuer, investor relations, SEC CIK, exchange listing, prospectus and fund document URLs use the same registry. No personal notes or keys appear in URLs. No external resources load until navigation.

Baseline uses outbound links only, no TradingView scraping, indicators, proprietary feeds, widgets, or official-integration claim. [TradingView policies](https://www.tradingview.com/policies/) must be reviewed before any future embed; that phase must document exact product/version, embed permission, attribution, user tracking/cookies, CSP, data rights and a consent/fallback path. Choose self-hosted Apache ECharts for own-data charts, under Apache-2.0 with applicable NOTICE and bundled subcomponent notices preserved; no TradingView chart library dependency. ### Normalization, budgets and freshness

Use stable internal instrument/listing IDs; tickers are time-bounded aliases, not primary keys. Store MIC, currency, timezone, share class, issuer/CIK, active/delisted dates and provider aliases. Avoid adding restricted identifier datasets without a license. Distinguish ETF inception, share-class inception, first exchange listing and corporate founding date. UTC timestamps internally; regular sessions use America/New_York and versioned exchange schedules including DST, holidays and half-days; UI can display America/Chicago without changing session boundaries.

Store unadjusted bars and action ledger, with separately derived split-adjusted price/volume and total-return series. Adjust OHLC consistently; inverse-adjust historical volume for share-count splits. Technicals use split-adjusted, non-dividend-adjusted prices. Returns use a separately documented reinvestment total-return series only when distributions are complete. A distribution is not a split. Freeze input/action versions for each signal and backtest; corrected data produces revisions, not edits to past evidence.

Source conflicts: prefer selected coherent price feed, issuer for ETF terms, SEC filing for reported accounting facts. Retain alternatives; do not average disagreeing prices. Flag same-time same-basis price differences over max(0.5%, two ticks), action-ratio disagreement, or mismatched units/dates. Quarantine unresolved action/price errors from rankings. Stale fallback must pass its own freshness test; browser timestamps never reset observation time.

Initial operating budgets: primary 60 requests/minute maximum, 2,000/day total, 20% held for retries/on-demand; Alpha Vantage 20/day plus 5 reserve; SEC 2 requests/second aggregate, 200/day routine plus separately scheduled initialization; issuer adapter at most one fetch/document/day unless expressly permitted otherwise. FRED 20/day; optional TVL 24/day. Token buckets shared across workers, count endpoint weights/pagination/retries. On 429 respect Retry-After; bounded exponential backoff with jitter (2, 4, 8, 16, 32 seconds, maximum five retries), circuit opens after five consecutive failures for 15 minutes; never retry entitlement errors blindly or evade limits with keys/IPs.

Capacity example (assumptions to verify): 100 instruments × 400 daily bars = 40,000 rows; at an entitled 10,000-row response ceiling, at least four pages, budget 20 calls for bootstrap because ordering/partitioning varies. 100 × 390 minute bars = 39,000 rows/day if all-market-minute ingestion later enabled; prefer incremental 15-minute polling and budget up to 10 calls/run × 26 runs = 260/day, plus corrections. Actual endpoint pagination may be less efficient; measure before enabling. Single-symbol APIs multiply calls and must reduce scope. Never schedule 100 symbols × 26 polls on a 25/day fallback.

EOD target: validated regular-session data by close + 90 minutes; unresolved at close + 120 minutes means delayed batch, no fabricated finality. Reconcile next morning. Optional 15-minute-delayed feed with 15-minute polling yields roughly 15–30 minutes plus processing age; UI shows measured age and provider delay separately. Intraday stale threshold: 45 minutes during open session; EOD stale: missing most recent completed session after publication deadline. Holidays/weekends do not count as missing sessions. ## 7. Technical-indicator definitions and scoring methodology

### Calculation conventions

All defaults below are proposed `methodology-v1`, deliberately explicit and to be validated, not empirical claims of edge. Inputs are completed regular-session split-adjusted OHLCV, chronological, one coherent feed, with no forward-filled missing bars. C/H/L/O/V denote close/high/low/open/volume; t is a closed bar. Daily is ranking default, weekly is confirmation; optional 60-minute is context only in v1. A week becomes complete only at the final exchange session close. Anchor 60-minute bars at 09:30 ET and retain the final 30-minute bar as a labeled partial-duration bar, excluding it from equal-duration 60-minute indicator series in v1. Never fill overnight intervals with zeros.

Fetch 400 daily bars where available; ranking requires ≥252 valid daily bars and 250 for stable EMA200 warmup. SMA n needs n bars; EMA n seeds with first n-bar SMA and α=2/(n+1), then recurses. Wilder RMA n seeds with arithmetic mean of first n values and recurses `(previous*(n−1)+current)/n`. Weekly indicators have independent warmups; missing weekly EMA200 does not suppress an otherwise valid daily view. Mark values before the configured warmup as provisional, not ranked. Zero denominators and non-finite results yield documented missing/flat states. Compute with double precision and round only for display; paper cash/quantities use decimal arithmetic. Every output contains params, engine version, input hash, as-of and eligibility diagnostics.

### Fibonacci and pivots

Daily pivot search: last 126 complete bars; weekly: 104 complete weeks; optional hourly: 120 complete full-duration bars. Configurable lookback daily 63–252, weekly 52–156, hourly 60–240. Daily/weekly symmetric pivot half-window k=3 (hourly k=2). High at index i must be the maximum H in [i−k,i+k], with earliest index winning ties; low similarly earliest minimum L. It becomes known only at close of i+k, not i. Reject a bar that simultaneously qualifies high and low as ambiguous. Require enough padding to confirm edge pivots; latest k bars never form confirmed pivots.

Scan confirmed pivots chronologically, retain an alternating sequence: consecutive highs keep the higher (earlier on tie), consecutive lows keep the lower. Accept opposite-type legs only if separation ≥5 bars daily/weekly (≥3 hourly) and absolute price range ≥2×ATR14 at confirmation; ignore subthreshold opposite pivots. A stronger same-type pivot may revise the developing anchor sequence; save each revision with known-at time. Pick the latest accepted opposite pair whose endpoints fall within lookback. No valid pair means Fibonacci unavailable, never arbitrary chart extrema. Store anchor index/price/type, pivot time, confirmed-at, range, direction, parameter version and invalidation history.

For a low L followed by high H, R=H−L: retracement at ratio r is `H−rR` for r in {0.236, 0.382, 0.500, 0.618, 0.786, 1.000}; r=0 is H. Two-anchor extensions are `L+eR` for e in {1.272,1.618,2.000,2.618}, with e=1.000 the prior high. For a high H followed by low L: retracement `L+rR`; extension `H−eR`. Label this as two-anchor range projection, not a three-point extension. All levels are geometric references, not evidence price must react. Nonpositive levels are omitted for positive-price securities. Reject R=0. A close beyond the origin invalidates the active leg for candidate planning; show it only as historical annotation until a new valid leg is confirmed.

Level distance: signed `C−level`, percent `100*(C−level)/C`, and ATR units `(C−level)/ATR14_daily`. Nearest uses absolute distance, tie prefers higher timeframe then smaller ratio. Confluence uses valid daily and weekly levels in the same split-adjustment basis: cluster sorted prices by complete-link distance ≤0.25 daily ATR, so the total cluster span cannot exceed the threshold. A cluster counts at most one vote per timeframe; multiple ratios from one swing do not multiply evidence. Display members, width, midpoint, timeframe count and distance. Nearest cluster within 0.5 ATR is “near”; two independent timeframes yield confluence, one is a single-level reference. Missing weekly history is disclosed, not invented. Optional hourly levels are visual-only in v1 scoring.

Confirmed pivots still change as new bars arrive. Provisional anchors may be shown dashed, excluded from scoring. Historical signals freeze the levels known at signal time; later pivot changes create a new snapshot and setup-review event. A chart can toggle “as known then” and “latest,” never repaint prior backtest entry logic.

Fundamentals use published-at SEC accessions, consistent units and fiscal periods. YoY revenue growth=(quarter revenue/prior-year comparable quarter−1) only with positive denominator. EPS growth=(EPS−prior EPS)/abs(prior EPS) if prior EPS>0; otherwise classify loss/profit transition and omit percentage. TTM sums four non-overlapping quarters; derive discrete fourth quarter from annual less first three only with compatible accounting context. Margin=TTM net income/TTM revenue (positive revenue). Trailing P/E=price/TTM diluted EPS only EPS>0; price/sales=dated market cap/TTM sales. Show age of shares used in market cap; do not confuse weighted-average EPS shares with current shares outstanding. Restatements are new versions and never backfilled into prior availability. Missing/custom XBRL tags require reviewed mappings; no guess that similar names are equivalent. Analyst targets/estimates excluded unless licensed. Dividend consistency needs complete cash history. Valuation is contextual, not proof of underpricing.

### Deterministic composite score

All component scores 0–100. Define clip(x)=min(1,max(0,x)). `p(x)` is same-snapshot midrank percentile within the same stock or ETF universe/profile among at least 20 eligible comparable instruments; never pool types to reach sample size; otherwise feature missing. Positive metrics increase p, inverse metrics use 1−p. Ties receive mean rank; a constant feature yields 0.5. Exact numeric score ties break on stable instrument ID, after liquidity as described in section 8.

Component recipes (equal-weight subfeatures unless stated):

- Trend T: mean of four 0/100 flags C>SMA50, SMA50>SMA200, SMA200 slope>0, +DI>−DI with ADX≥20. Neutral/mixed flags score 0, no inferred trend strength.
- Momentum M: RSI suitability + normalized MACD histogram + excess 63-day return. RSI suitability=100*clip(1−abs(RSI−55)/30); MACD=100*clip(0.5+hist/(0.2*ATR)); RS=100*p(excess63). Bearish divergence deducts 15 points from M (floor 0); bullish divergence is displayed but no bonus in v1.
- Fibonacci F: 70*clip(1−nearest valid support cluster distance in ATR/0.5)+30 if that near cluster contains daily and weekly levels, capped 100. A support cluster midpoint must be ≤C and active; if no near cluster, F=0; if no valid pivot history, F missing. Resistance is shown but not awarded support credit.
- Participation/liquidity L: mean of 100*p(log ADDV20), 100*clip((RVOLday−0.5)/1.5), and 100*clip((OBV10normalized+1)/2). RVOL>5 is anomaly review, not unlimited positive points.
- Volatility quality Q: 100*clip(1−abs(ATR%−2)/4). ETF equity defaults same function; Bond ETFs not in default equity candidate scoring. A high Q means within a chosen movement band, not safe.
- Fundamentals U, stocks: mean percentile revenue YoY growth, EPS YoY growth where meaningful, net margin, and dividend consistency if dividend category; non-dividend stocks omit dividend feature as structurally inapplicable. Growth ETFs: weighted underlying growth fields and profitability coverage. Dividend ETFs: three equal factors distribution-year continuity (100×paid complete years/5), 100*p(three-year regular dividend CAGR), and 100*(1−p(top-ten concentration)). Core ETFs: 50% inverse expense percentile, 50% inverse absolute tracking-difference percentile using comparable benchmarks. Dividend cuts remain explicit warnings. Missing inputs reduce completeness.
- Valuation/context V: stocks mean inverse positive P/E and P/S percentiles within sector (at least 20 peers); otherwise unavailable, not an unfair cross-sector rank. ETFs mean inverse expense percentile and inverse issuer-comparable portfolio P/E where available; no portfolio P/E for bond funds. No high-yield-is-better shortcut.

Include total applicable and available weight. ## 8. Daily board and five-candidate pipeline

### Universe and board

Store daily universe manifest and reason for membership/exclusion. Initial target 60 US common stocks (NYSE/Nasdaq/NYSE American) and 40 ordinary ETFs (also NYSE Arca/Cboe BZX), subject to permitted reference coverage. Candidate stocks price≥$5, market cap≥$1 billion with shares observation no older than 135 days; ETFs AUM≥$100 million, unlevered equity exposure, expense/inception/type verified. All candidates require consolidated ADDV20≥$10 million, ADV20≥100,000 shares, ≥252 daily bars, positive valid ATR, ATR% 0.5–6%, no unresolved actions/halts, complete last 20 sessions and ≥98% coverage of last 252 expected sessions. Known median spread >30 bps excludes; absent spread is labeled missing optional data. These are configurable research exclusions, not safety guarantees.

Stock boards require common-stock type, price≥$5, market cap≥$300 million with shares age≤135 days, ADDV20≥$5 million, ≥21 valid sessions, valid prior close/current snapshot. ETF boards require ordinary unlevered ETF type, price≥$5, AUM≥$50 million with source age≤45 days, known expense/inception, ≥21 sessions since listing, ADDV20≥$2 million and valid prior close/current snapshot. Stock market-cap rules never apply to ETFs; ETF boards warn about duplicate exposure without suppressing legitimate fund listings. Expense≤1% and AUM≥$100 million apply to ETF candidates, not board inclusion. No All-assets ranking or full-market claim. Board does not require bullish signals or fundamentals. New listings lacking history appear in a separate insufficient-history list. Penny/microcap and OTC securities are excluded from default rankings with reasons available.

Gainers rank descending `100*(P−Cprev)/Cprev`; losers ascending, using prior regular-session close adjusted into today's share basis for splits (not dividend total return). Dollar change=P−Cprev. Only positive movers enter gainers and negative movers losers; zeros are not filler. At EOD P is validated regular close; intraday P is last eligible regular-session trade/bar close at or before common cutoff. Quote timestamp can predate cutoff and must be visible. Board primary volume rank is cumulative regular-session **share volume**, with a distinct **dollar-volume** board where permitted, carrying its own rank identity. Dollar volume=sum permitted bar VWAP×volume if available; otherwise sum close×volume labeled estimate, never last price×whole-day volume disguised as exact turnover.

Rows contain symbol/name/type/exchange, price, % and absolute change, share/dollar volume and basis, ADV20, ADDV20, RVOL, timestamp, session, source/feed and delay. Ties: primary metric, then dollar volume descending, then stable ID. Display ten slots with top min(10,eligible count) rows; empty slots explain the reason. Disabled dollar-volume boards show rights/data-unavailable status. Snapshot includes session date, requested cutoff, oldest/newest observation, generated time, eligible/expected counts and source coverage. Do not combine IEX volume with consolidated prior-day averages.

Pre-market and after-hours, if enabled, occupy distinct boards: last extended-session price versus preceding regular close, and extended-session-only volume; never blend with regular leaders. Freeze regular board at close; new extended-session observations don't rewrite it. Holidays show last completed session with next session time. Halts show badge and last observation outside active ranks when confirmed; absence of prints alone means potentially stale, not a confirmed halt. Unknown halt feed means “halt status unavailable.”

Bad ticks: validate positive OHLC, L≤O/C≤H, H≥L, nonnegative integer volume, timestamps/session/feed and duplicate keys. Large moves >30% or >10 prior ATR trigger review against actions/alternate permitted feed; they are suspicious, not automatically impossible. Splits/reverse splits require action reconciliation and comparable prior close; unresolved rows quarantine. An official action does not waive price/liquidity criteria. Revisions produce a new board version and retain previous snapshot.

### Publication pipeline

1. Determine completed exchange session and common information cutoff; acquire immutable raw/source manifests within budget.
2. Normalize listings/actions/feed/session; validate and quarantine; calculate universe coverage before publishing anything.
3. Produce four stock boards and four ETF boards from type-specific snapshots even if candidacy is unavailable. Dollar-volume boards can be explicitly disabled for rights/inputs. Publish each type atomically, independent of the other type succeeding.
4. Compute daily and completed-week indicators, PIT fundamentals and event coverage; record per-instrument exclusions and c.
5. Apply mandatory filters, score version and thresholds; generate proposed entry plans (section 9). Wait-only candidates can qualify for research but must be distinct from confirmed states.
6. Select up to five stocks and up to five ETFs independently using the rules above. No combined ranking, percentile pool or cross-type backfill.
7. Within each type sort cards by final score descending, ADDV descending, stable ID. Save quotas/relaxations, all scored/excluded instruments and immutable inputs/config in that type's transaction.
8. Each type publishes `status=success` only with five real qualifying instruments; `degraded` with 0–4 and usable data (reason `insufficient_qualifiers`); or `data_unavailable` if required source gates fail. Slots carry reason codes. Historical batches remain separately dated, never reused as new. Five wait-only candidates are possible and disclosed. One type can succeed while the other is unavailable.
9. Evaluate follow-up rules after publication and after subsequent validated batches. Never delete a losing candidate or reset its original timestamp.

Every card/detail supplies price/time/delay, category/profile, score components/coverage, Fibonacci anchors/levels, RSI, MA distances/trend, ATR/regime, liquidity/feed, fundamental periods, event warnings, relevant news links, inclusion/exclusion reasons, setup invalidation, review horizon, risk/reward assumptions, not-personalized-advice disclaimer and a conditional plan. “Current price” is latest observed price, with signal-time price separately shown. Daily candidates remain fixed for the session; updates affect state and may invalidate them, not silently swap the list.

### News ingestion and selection pipeline

Approved RSS/API metadata → rights/publisher/HTTPS URL/time validation → canonical URL and verified entities → deduplication → overview/per-asset selection → context flags/links. This branch cannot mutate technical scoring. No article body or sentiment inference is required.

Preserve publisher canonical URL and original URL; strip known tracking parameters without dropping article identity. Exact canonical URL is first duplicate key; explicit syndication ID next. Otherwise normalized title + overlapping verified entities + publication within 24 hours forms a reviewable duplicate group; similar headlines alone are insufficient. Prefer original source/release within group, retaining alternate links. Revisions preserve original published_at and track updated_at; retrieved_at never makes old news fresh.

Overview eligibility: permitted title display, nonempty publisher/title, valid source URL, publication within last 48 hours and not in future (over five-minute clock tolerance quarantined), verified tracked-asset or broad-market relevance. Sort publication descending, then primary-source priority, then stable ID; one item per duplicate group. “Top” means newest relevant permitted coverage, not trade significance. Select five if available, otherwise explicit vacancies/reasons with last fetch and coverage. Distinguish zero fresh stories from outage; neither implies no market events. Asset default lookback 30 days, paginated; official filings remain separately grouped. Unknown publication times appear only in dated-unknown source links, not current Top 5.

Match source IDs/CIK or curated issuer aliases plus validated listing; ambiguous ticker words never auto-link. A join table allows one story to affect multiple stocks/ETFs/commodities/crypto. Underlying-stock news is not automatically relevant to every ETF; optional indirect-exposure links must show holdings date/weight. Relevance notes are neutral factual templates or reviewed original text, not copied article summaries or trade advice. Retractions/corrections preserve audit and update context badges.

## 9. Candidate entry/exit/follow-up alert model and paper trading

Paper accounts/intents/journals, alerts and outcomes require `asset_section=stocks|etfs`. Separate default simulated cash ledgers ($100,000 each), routes, balances and performance reports; combined analytics later cannot mask type outcomes. Validate signal/instrument/account/alert section equality server-side. ETF events are distributions, index rebalances and fund changes; sponsor-company earnings do not trigger stock earnings gates. Underlying earnings exposure stays context unless a validated feature is enabled. Commodity/crypto views have no MVP candidate or simulated execution pipeline.

V1 supports one long-only pullback research template. Other attractive ranked assets show “Wait for confirmation” without invented precision. Eligible template: bullish daily regime, active upward Fibonacci leg, nearest retracement support cluster below/at C within 0.5 ATR, no benchmark bearish caution or unresolved imminent event warning. Freeze setup ATR A, support midpoint S and cluster lower bound B at signal time.

Candidate zone `[S−0.25A, S+0.25A]`, restricted to positive prices. Structural invalidation I=B−0.75A. Reject a plan whose upper-zone risk `(zone_upper−I)` is <0.5A or >2.5A, or I≤0; show wait-state reason. Confirm only on a later completed daily bar whose low touches zone, close reclaims S and close exceeds prior close, with RVOL≥1.0 and no intervening invalidation. A close below I before confirmation invalidates; intrabar breach separately warns. Pending signal expires after three regular sessions unless replaced by an explicitly new dated setup. Newly confirmed setup allows next-session hypothetical entry only if observed next open is inside the original zone; otherwise “entry missed/reassess.” Delayed end-of-day data cannot provide a timely market-entry instruction.

Target T1 is nearest frozen confirmed resistance above zone upper, including prior swing high; T2 next higher resistance or 1.272 extension. If unavailable, show no structural target; optional `E+2*(E−I)` displayed as a hypothetical 2R reference, not predicted price. Require T1 above entry and conservative net R:R≥1.5 for actionable research state using zone upper as E. Net R:R=`(T1−E−estimated roundtrip cost/share)/(E−I+estimated roundtrip cost/share)`; specify commissions, slippage, gap limitations. Inadequate ratio → wait-state. Show geometric references even when no plan qualifies.

Holding horizon 5–20 sessions is an expectation for review, not a minimum lock-in. Stop/invalidation reference I, T1/T2, two consecutive closes below EMA20, known upcoming earnings review, and 20-session time expiry are independent exit/review conditions. V1 simulated default exits entire position at first stop or T1 or 20-session next-open time exit; T2/EMA20 are research alerts unless user explicitly selects a separate journal plan. Trailing reference can only tighten (max prior stop, highest completed close since fill−2×current ATR); opt-in new paper plan version, never widen retrospectively. Stops do not guarantee execution at I; overnight gaps can lose more.

States: `wait_confirmation → confirmed → expired|invalidated|superseded`; an executed paper intent transitions separately `draft → pending → open → closed|cancelled`. Setup can become stale from any nonterminal state; retain preceding state and suppress price alerts until data recovers. No “BUY NOW” baseline label. If later configured, it must mean a research alert meeting stated conditions and show time, delay, pending confirmation/expiry and invalidation; never unconditional execution language.

Research presets: Standard as above; Conservative ADDV≥$25m, ATR%≤4, avoid confirmed earnings within five sessions, require verified event coverage and net R:R≥2; no higher-risk preset in MVP. Users may edit screens and inspect excluded assets but hard type/data constraints remain. Presets do not ask age/income/net worth or imply suitable sizing. Paper risk fraction optional default 0.5% of hypothetical equity, maximum 1% in preset; size=floor(min(risk_budget/(E−I+cost), available_cash/E)), bounded by liquidity participation. This is a simulation assumption, not a recommendation for real capital.

Alerts: support/zone observation, confirmation, invalidation/breach, target/exit/time expiry, MA crossover, RSI regime transition/divergence, ADX regime change, pivot replacement, earnings at five/two sessions, ex-dividend date, missing expected bar, and source outage/recovery. Evaluate on validated completed data, default EOD; intraday only when explicitly enabled. Each rule has instrument, parameters/config version, timeframe, source freshness requirement, crossing direction, hysteresis (0.1 ATR for price rearm), cooldown (one session default), expiration, channel and timezone. Event dedupe key=rule+signal version+bar/event ID+transition; persistent outbox delivery retries separate from evaluations. Never send retroactive crossing as current; backlog events say “detected late.” Alerts may be delayed, missed, unavailable while ThinkCentre is offline, or arrive after a move. They are not protective orders.

Paper ledger is entirely local to hi buy.com, starting configurable hypothetical $100,000, no real connection. User creates intents; simulator observes future bars, never creates a fill on the signal bar. Default daily fills: next eligible open plus adverse 10 bps slippage per side; base commissions $0 configurable; sensitivity 5/25/50 bps and $1/order. Quantity capped at 1% previous ADV20 and 10% observed execution-bar volume where available; otherwise cap daily participation and mark fill coarse. Unfilled remainder persists one session then cancels; no magical unlimited liquidity.

Limit intent fills only if later bar range reaches limit, with conservative adverse-slippage check inside limit; gaps above buy limit do not fill. Stop-market exits: if open<I use open minus slippage, else if low≤I use I minus slippage. Target-limit exits conservatively at T1 when reached, not favorable gap price. If same daily bar reaches stop and target without intrabar ordering, stop first; label ambiguity, count sensitivity variants. For a pending intrabar fill with same-bar exits, assume adverse ordering or mark indeterminate, never silently choose profitable ordering. Time-exit uses next eligible open after 20 completed holding sessions. Corporate actions adjust paper quantities/references and book distributions on payment date where known. Cash insufficient prevents fill; mark-to-market uses delayed observed prices with stale flags.

Journal captures immutable signal time/input/config, user thesis/scenarios, intent time, active/stale status, modeled fill time/price/size, costs/model version, changes and reasons, dividends, unrealized/realized P&L, MAE/MFE, R multiple and follow-up outcomes. User edits append revisions; no erasing original losing evidence. Report alerts-only outcomes separately from user-selected paper trades to expose selection bias.

## 12. Testing, backtesting, and validation strategy

Indicator unit tests use hand-calculated small vectors plus independent trusted reference calculations with specified seeding tolerance (absolute 1e−8 for ordinary magnitudes, relative 1e−8 for large values). Test constant/rising/falling prices, RSI zero loss/gain, MACD seed, tied DM, ADX flat data, Bollinger population deviation, OBV equal close, low history, missing sessions, zero volume, splits, dividends, reverse splits and extended-session boundaries. Fibonacci tests cover ties, confirmation lag, threshold acceptance, alternating anchors, invalidation, no valid swing, complete-link clustering, changing pivots and unchanged historical snapshots. Prefix-invariance test: appending future bars must not change earlier as-known signals.

Provider contracts: replay sanitized permitted responses for auth failure/entitlement, 429, pagination, empty/partial bars, action revisions, schema changes, conflicting timestamps, currencies and units. Live tests opt-in, bounded and read-only, not required in CI. Every fixture has provenance/license/date/hash and explicit real-replay versus synthetic-test label. No provider key in logs or recordings.

Ranking tests: weights sum to 100, monotonic subfeatures where defined, missing coverage penalty, category hybrid recipe, zero vs N/A, earnings unknown penalty, deterministic ties, no duplicate classes/exposure, five-success/insufficient/outage semantics, concentration reasons, snapshot consistency and split-comparable board returns. Property tests prevent NaN, nonpositive stock/ETF prices and future information from reaching a securities ranking; commodity futures use their distinct signed-price validation.

Integration tests: idempotent jobs/restarts, transactional publication, retry budgets/circuit breaker, stale fallback, session calendars/DST/half-days, outbox duplicate suppression, authorization isolation, paper decimal/cash conservation and action accounting. Link tests use NASDAQ:AAPL, verified AMEX:SPY, Berkshire class punctuation via explicitly registered alias, an international dual listing, numeric symbol, preferred share, renamed/delisted symbol, unknown exchange, invalid script URL and on-chain contract with no mapping. Unverified examples must yield unavailable rather than invented URLs.

Accessibility/E2E: keyboard-only discover→compare→asset→source drawer→notes→paper flow; mobile 360/390/768/1440 widths; screen-reader chart table equivalence, focus restoration, contrast, no color-only states, offline/delayed/empty states. Use browser automation and automated accessibility checks plus manual keyboard/screen-reader checks. Performance targets on ThinkCentre after baseline measurement: cached overview p95<500 ms API, interactive core content <3 s on simulated midrange mobile/network, EOD calculation for 100 instruments <5 min excluding provider wait. These are acceptance targets to measure, not promises before hardware inspection.

### Revision-specific acceptance tests

- Four independent routes/headings/filter schemas/typed responses/methodologies/health. Crypto outage cannot block stock/ETF publication. Reject ETF rows/config in stock boards/candidates and vice versa; no mixed-rank API.
- All eight board headings, independent share/dollar ordering, full metadata, type-specific filters, regular/extended sessions, rights-disabled dollar volume and ten-slot shortages. Nine stocks never gain an ETF/stale tenth row.
- Independent candidate outcomes: five/five, five/two, zero/five, one unavailable, both unavailable. No backfill; test issuer duplicates, ETF overlap/hybrid/tag-quota relaxation. News changes cannot alter numeric score or frozen candidates.
- BTC/ETH/SOL price fields, observation timestamps, market-cap nulls, aggregate/venue labels, ticker collisions, decimals, weekends and stale/outage states. No stock-close comparison or native-coin APR.
- Commodity unit/currency/period mapping, spot/futures/ETF separation, contract month/expiry, roll discontinuity, negative futures price, mismatched curve times, unknown contango, monthly benchmark labels and missing-source cards.
- News: duplicate URLs/syndication versus similar distinct events; stale/future/missing timestamps; wrong ticker matches; missing publisher/source; malformed/javascript URLs and malicious markup; paywall/link-only items; one story linked to multiple assets; indirect ETF exposure; retractions; rights-disabled feeds; outage versus no fresh stories; five/fewer-than-five states. Correct asset links/attribution; no bodies stored/exposed.
- Paper/alerts: cross-section account/signal rejected, separate balances/history, no sponsor-earnings stock gate on ETFs. Mobile E2E visits all four sections, both candidate groups, Top 5 Market News, asset News unavailable and official-link fallback.

Evaluate stock and ETF profiles/universes independently with separate coverage and benchmarks. Commodity/crypto are descriptive; news is context, with no news-driven strategy performance claim.

### Evaluation design

Separate (A) pipeline correctness/replay, (B) retrospective descriptive study, and (C) credible out-of-sample strategy evaluation. A biased dataset may support A and carefully labeled B, never be represented as C. The app always states backtests cannot guarantee future performance.

Persist data bitemporally: effective market/report period and known/available time plus ingestion version. Signal at session t uses only bars finalized and facts/constituents/actions/pivots known by publication cutoff; earliest simulated fill is after actual publication, normally next session open. A release after the scheduled batch cutoff first enters the next batch. When a source supplies only a filing date, use next-session availability conservatively and flag timestamp uncertainty. Fundamentals without historical availability are excluded from PIT tests, not assigned fiscal-period end. ALFRED vintages required for macro-revision-sensitive tests. Today's split-adjusted series cannot silently change historic absolute price eligibility; reconstruct contemporaneous price/share basis and action knowledge.

Universe bias: archive additions/removals and delisted securities from the start, including ticker changes, failed firms, merger proceeds and terminal outcomes where permitted. Obtain a legally usable historical membership/delisting dataset if feasible; no $0 assumption of complete coverage. Current 100-symbol history is explicitly survivorship- and selection-biased. Do not pretend it represents the whole market or fill delisted prices by carrying them forward. Missing terminal proceeds leave outcomes unresolved; publish counts and pessimistic sensitivity separately. If PIT universe/delistings cannot be obtained, report forward paper results and limited retrospective diagnostics, not validated profitable strategy claims.

Walk-forward proposed design: at least five years where available; rolling 36-month training, 6-month validation, next 6-month untouched test, step six months. Purge training/validation labels whose holding intervals overlap later folds and impose 20-session embargo matching max holding horizon. All rank normalization uses only that date's eligible peer set; any learned threshold/calibration fits training only. Keep a final 12-month holdout outside tuning and document all attempted variants, including failed ones. Scarce history → fewer folds and explicit limitation, not random shuffled cross-validation. Parameters in section 7 remain hypotheses until evaluated; freeze a new config before starting a new forward study.

Run identical candidate/entry/exit rules and cost model as paper simulation; no same-bar hindsight confirmation/fills, perfect lows, unlimited liquidity, or always-available shorts. Include commissions, adverse spread/slippage, volume participation, gaps, holidays, halts, dividends, delisting treatment and cash drag. Test stop-first same-bar ambiguity and worse slippage scenarios. Price-limit touch alone is weak fill evidence: conservative fill rules and lower-frequency-data limitation must appear in results. Separate gross/net and price/total-return results. No real order execution code required.

Benchmarks: total-return SPY for broad US equity; appropriate issuer/index or investable asset-class benchmark for ETF category; equal-weight eligible-universe buy-and-hold with same dates/cost convention; cash baseline with documented available rate or explicitly 0%. Use matched observation windows/currency and exposure/cash reporting. Do not compare an IEX strategy price series to differently timed consolidated close returns without reconciliation.

Report number of candidates/trades/unresolved outcomes, coverage, total and annualized return (CAGR only windows≥1 year), maximum peak-to-trough drawdown, win/hit rate (#positive net closed trades / #resolved closed trades), mean win/loss and expectancy `pwin*avgwin−ploss*abs(avgloss)`, expectancy in R, profit factor (undefined if no losses), turnover (annualized sum absolute traded notional / mean equity, labeled two-way), market exposure, holding duration, MAE/MFE, volatility and Sharpe (sqrt252×mean daily excess return / sample SD), Sortino with explicit downside threshold, worst gaps and performance by regime/category. Zero-variance ratios undefined, not infinity. Use block-bootstrap uncertainty intervals preserving serial dependence and report sample size; no probability-of-profit score extrapolation.

Release validation gates are data correctness, reproducibility, rights and honest disclosures, not a minimum profitable backtest. Before declaring strategy evidence, require untouched evaluation, adverse-cost sensitivity and multiple regimes; publish negative results. Run a minimum 20-session forward operational pilot for failures/freshness, expressly too short to establish investment efficacy.

## 13. Security, privacy, disclaimers, and operational risks

Private authenticated app with single-owner authorization on every API and job-triggering route; secure HttpOnly SameSite sessions, CSRF defense for mutations, TLS remotely, password/passkey via vetted auth package chosen at implementation. Secrets only in server environment/protected secret store, excluded from Git/client bundles/logs; provider adapters allowlist data hosts and read operations. Reject arbitrary remote fetch URLs to prevent SSRF. No order SDK/API, trading host, wallets, or brokerage tokens required.

Validate symbol/filter/note inputs, parameterize SQL, sanitize rendered Markdown and disallow executable HTML; CSP blocks unapproved scripts/frames. External links are validated registry data. Per-owner API limit proposed 60 reads/minute and 10 mutations/minute, refresh one/instrument/minute plus provider global budget; pagination/range limits stop unbounded queries. Sign cursors or validate server-side. Dependencies locked and checked; source rights flags enforced server-side, not only hidden buttons.

Notes and simulated balances are private. No third-party analytics by default, no advertising pixels or externally fetched logos on asset pages. Data minimization; owner JSON/CSV export with source rights checks, deletion of personal notes/accounts and defined backup expiry (proposed 30 days). Audit records avoid storing note bodies unnecessarily. Encrypted daily backups, restore drill, proposed recovery-point target 24 hours and recovery-time target one working day for private MVP. ThinkCentre power/network outages and sleep suspend alerts; visible worker heartbeat and last evaluation time.

Structured logs/traces: run/source/session/snapshot IDs, latency, retry/quota/quarantine/coverage, missing indicators, stale alerts and paper inconsistencies. Dashboard warns on job heartbeat >10 minutes during scheduled work, snapshot SLA miss, schema drift, abnormal null rate, repeated 429 and failed backup. Never log credentials, full private notes, or licensed payloads beyond allowed retention. Source revocation disables affected fetching/display/export per terms and records audit reason.

Required text near candidates: “Research candidate, not individualized financial advice. This setup may lose money. Prices and alerts may be delayed or incomplete. Review the source time, assumptions and invalidation conditions.” Near scores: “Methodology alignment score; not a probability of profit.” Near paper ledger: “Simulation only. Modeled fills and costs may differ materially from real execution.” Near evaluation: “Past and backtested results do not guarantee future results; see data and bias limitations.” Near yield: “Distribution yield is not guaranteed total return; distributions may include return of capital.” No daily-income claims, profit streak gamification, unconditional buy buttons, or misleading green certainty indicators.

Material risks: stale/ambiguous news, syndication duplicates, ticker misattribution, commodity rights gaps, crypto aggregate/venue mismatch, negative/rolled futures, and insufficient free redistribution rights; provider plan changes; partial venue volume; scarce PIT histories; issuer metadata maintenance; action corrections; sparse ETF fundamentals/events; correlated technical factors; parameter overfit; external-link changes; server downtime; delayed alerts; user overreliance. Each has a visible failure state and a release gate or reduced capability described above. A disclaimer does not remedy a misleading computation or unlicensed feed.

## 16. Open questions and explicit non-goals

Resolve first: (1) private Jay-only versus eventual public display; (2) willingness to obtain a free market-data account without brokerage use; (3) approved commodity series and crypto/news source permissions; (4) whether a tracked-universe board meets the requirement or full-market coverage is mandatory; (5) legal replay distribution source; (6) exact initial universe and issuer-adapter maintenance budget. These determine live feasibility, not merely cosmetic choices.

Before later phases: intraday need versus delayed EOD sufficiency; notification channel and uptime expectation; remote hosting; international listings/currencies; independent stock/ETF universe membership; preferred risk preset; benchmark mappings; ETF overlap data availability; whether on-chain scope is actually wanted; public redistribution and derived-score permissions; defensible delisted/PIT dataset availability. Recheck all provider terms at activation, record evidence, and expose unresolved source capabilities rather than silently enabling them.

Explicit non-goals: profits or daily-income guarantees; individualized financial advice; autonomous or manual real-money order execution through hi buy.com; brokerage connection/funding; wallets/signing/DeFi transactions; options/shorts/leverage; copied analyst research/news; TradingView scraping/proprietary-data ingestion/restricted embeds; a guaranteed complete market terminal; a fabricated exactly-five list during outages; unvalidated probability-of-profit scores; hidden paid subscriptions; implementation/scaffolding/package installs/keys in this planning run.

The implementation agent should follow these defaults, preserve uncertainty and immutable evidence, and raise only decisions that block a dependent capability. It must not quietly broaden the universe, weaken data gates, reinterpret yields, invent missing fundamentals, or convert research plans into executable trading instructions.
